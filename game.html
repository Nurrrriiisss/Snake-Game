Questions

Short answers about the game.

Is it free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy and no account to create.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs in your browser on desktop, tablet and phone.

Where are my scores saved?

Only on this device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, so clearing your browser data also clears your best scores.

Does the game collect personal data?

No. The game has no accounts, analytics or ads. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which is the only outside request it makes.

The game paused by itself. Why?

It pauses when you scroll the board out of view or switch to another tab, so you don't lose a run by accident.
